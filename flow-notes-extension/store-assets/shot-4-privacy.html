Flow Notes
Private by design

Your highlights never touch our database.

They go straight into your note. Our server keeps only a small index of which notes exist and where they live.

Our server keeps

  • ·A pseudonymous account id — not your name or email
  • ·Note titles, and where each note lives
  • ·When each note was created and updated

Never stored

  • ×The text you highlight
  • ×The pages you read
  • ×What your notes say
  • ×Analytics or tracking of any kind
Settings → Delete my data from the server erases every record we hold, in one click.
